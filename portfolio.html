What the test showed

Learnings for the full campaign

Purpose-made creatives win for leads. The new Welcoming Sponsors ad brought 2.6 times more leads than the boosted existing post at the same spend.

Tight targeting kept leads relevant. A 20 km radius around Doha focused spend on businesses that can exhibit.
